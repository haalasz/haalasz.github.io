# About Me

Since 2023 I have been working at the Institute of One Health, Faculty of Health Sciences, University of Debrecen, as a researcher and PhD student. My work focuses on bacteriophages - why they work, how they work and how they could help solve global health problems. During my studies I focused on antibiotic resistance and I am now in the fortunate position to work on one of the possible solutions to this global problem.

Outside of my research, I enjoy playing football with friends, playing games (Football Manager, League of Legends) and taking photos.
