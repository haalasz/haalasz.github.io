# About Me

From 2023 to 2026, I worked at the Institute of One Health, Faculty of Health Sciences, University of Debrecen, as a researcher and PhD student. My work focused on bacteriophages - why they work, how they work, and how they could help solve global health problems. My current focus is on quality assurance, data integrity, and regulated data management.
